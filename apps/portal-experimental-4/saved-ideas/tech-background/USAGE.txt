Saved idea: Smooth technical background (Experimental-4)

What it is
- A smooth gradient-mesh background with a slow rotating sheen, light grain, drifting circuit lines, and gentle mist.
- Mouse movement updates CSS vars --mx/--my for subtle parallax in gradients.

Where it came from
- CSS originally implemented in: apps/portal-experimental-4/src/app/globals.css
- JS parallax originally implemented in: apps/portal-experimental-4/src/app/page.tsx

How to reuse (quick)
1) Paste background.css contents into your globals.css (or import it).
2) In your hero left panel container:
   - Add className="tech-bg"
   - Add children overlays near the top of that container:
     <div className="tech-mesh" />
     <div className="tech-lines" />
     <div className="atmospheric-mist" />
3) Add the mousemove handler:
   - Either copy the logic from page.tsx or use the useCssVarParallax hook.
4) Keep your hero content (orb + speech bubble) wrapped in a z-10 container.

Notes
- Respects prefers-reduced-motion if you keep the checks.
- All animations are opacity/transform/background-position (performance-friendly).
